# 4P Index coding — Senegal environmental regulatory framework (2001–2023)

This folder contains completed **Plastic Pollution Policy Index (4P Index)** coding tables
(Columns A–W, per the *4P Index — Policy Coding Prompt v2*) for Senegal's environmental code and its
implementing instruments, produced as downloadable artifacts. It now covers **five** documents:

| Document | Files | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Loi n° 2001-01 du 15 janvier 2001 (law) | `senegal_loi_2001-01_*` | Repealed, replaced by the 2023 law |
| Décret n° 2001-282 du 12 avril 2001 (implementing decree) | `senegal_decret_2001-282_*` | Implements the (repealed) 2001 law; remains applicable pending replacement decrees for the 2023 law, to the extent compatible |
| Norme NS 05-061 + Arrêté Interministériel (2001, technical standard) | `senegal_norme_ns05061_*` | Wastewater discharge standard, made binding by ministerial order; complements the decree above |
| Arrêtés ministériels n° 9468–9472 MJEHP-DEEC du 28 novembre 2001 (EIA procedure) | `senegal_arretes_eia2001_*` | Still in force per the task brief; implements the decree's EIA titles |
| **Loi n° 2023-15 du 02 août 2023 (law)** | `senegal_loi_2023-15_*` | **Current, in-force Code de l'Environnement** |

## Document verification (2023 law)

The user supplied a PDF (`Loi-no-2023-15-du-02-aou-t-2023-portant-Code-de-lEnvironnement_d106.pdf`).
It is a scanned image PDF (no embedded text layer), so it was OCR'd (`tesseract`, French model) at
300 dpi to confirm its identity. The OCR output matches the official publication: **Journal Officiel
de la République du Sénégal, 88e année, n° 7660, samedi 16 septembre 2023**, containing "*Loi n°
2023-15 du 02 août 2023 portant Code de l'Environnement*", adopted by the National Assembly on 7 June
2023, reviewed by the Constitutional Council (decision n° 5/C/2023 of 12 July 2023), and expressly
repealing Loi n° 2001-01 (its Art. 255: *"La présente loi abroge et remplace la loi n° 2001-01 du 15
janvier 2001 portant Code de l'Environnement."*). **This is confirmed to be the correct/right document
for this coding task.** Given the OCR quality on the scanned upload was noisy, the actual coding below
was performed against a clean full-text mirror of the same law (primature.sn), cross-checked line-for-
line against the OCR of the uploaded PDF for the exposé des motifs and adoption/promulgation clauses.

## What was coded — Décret n° 2001-282 (2001), the implementing decree

**Policy coded:** *Décret n° 2001-282 du 12 avril 2001 portant application du Code de l'Environnement*
— 86 articles across 6 titles (classified installations; EIA; water pollution; water policing; air
pollution; noise pollution), signed at Dakar on 12 April 2001 by President Abdoulaye Wade and PM
Mame Madior Boye. Source: user-uploaded PDF (clean, machine-readable text, confirmed to match
https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/sen37192.pdf as supplied in the task brief) — **confirmed correct**.

**Date-discrepancy resolution:** earlier task briefs referenced "*the Code de l'Environnement...du 12
avril 2001*", which did not match either the 2001 Law (dated 15 January 2001) or the 2023 Law (dated
2 August 2023). Coding this document resolves that discrepancy: **12 April 2001 is this decree's own
date**, not the Law's — the original source database entry evidently conflated the Law's Journal
Officiel citation with its implementing decree's date.

**Plastics relevance:** like both laws, this decree never mentions "plastique" (confirmed by full-text
search). All 7 coded instruments qualify under filter criteria (b)/(c) — they operationalise the ICPE
permitting regime, EIA procedure, water-discharge standards, pollution taxes, marine/coastal dumping
prohibitions, and air-emission/incineration-equipment standards that plastics production, effluent and
waste-incineration activities are subject to in practice. The noise-pollution title (Titre VI, Art.
R83-R86) was reviewed but **excluded** from the coding table as it does not meet any of the three
Plastics Relevance Filter criteria.

**Policy-type note:** this is an executive/presidential decree (issued after Conseil d'Etat review, not
enacted by Parliament), so it is coded 0.75 rather than 1 — a different Column G value from the parent
laws it implements, per Coding Rule 6.

**Summary of results (2001 Decree):**
- **Policy-level score (Column O)** = (G + I + K + M) / 4 = (0.75 + 1 + 0.75 + 0.5) / 4 = **0.75**
  (executive decree; 14 sectors ⇒ 7+ bracket; 4 of 5 life-cycle phases addressed — production,
  recycling, disposal, environmental leakage, but not consumption; taxes/funding mentioned but the
  ring-fencing declaration itself lives in the parent law, not this decree, so M = 0.5 rather than 1).
- **7 instrument rows** coded: the ICPE authorisation/declaration procedure, the EIA procedure, water
  effluent discharge standards, the pollution-tax computation mechanism, marine/coastal water-police
  prohibitions and enforcement, fixed-installation air-pollution control and special protection zones,
  and incineration/combustion-equipment technical standards and periodic inspection.
- Instrument scores (Column V) range from **0.45** (EIA procedure) to **0.875** (ICPE regime; marine/
  coastal dumping prohibitions; air-pollution control; incineration-equipment standards). Every
  instrument in this decree is coded **in force** (S=1); no purely-contingent, unexercised enabling
  power was identified as its own separate instrument.

## What was coded — Norme NS 05-061 (2001), wastewater discharge standard

**Policy coded:** *Norme sénégalaise NS 05-061 — Eaux usées : Normes de rejet* (July 2001), adopted by
the ISN's environment technical committee (ISN/CT5), together with the accompanying **Arrêté
Interministériel fixant les conditions de rejets des eaux usées** (signed jointly by the Ministers of
Youth/Environment/Public Hygiene and of Handicrafts/Industry; undated in the extracted text but citing
decrees up to 30 August 2001) that makes the standard legally binding. Source: user-uploaded PDF
(Source: Direction de l'Environnement et des Etablissements Classés / Institut Sénégalais de
Normalisation), consistent with the task-brief URL.

**Relationship to the other coded documents:** the standard's own Avant-Propos states it "vient
compléter le décret n° 2001-282 du 12 avril 2001" — i.e. it is the detailed technical annex to the
implementing decree already coded in this series, and Art. 7 of its implementing Arrêté cross-references
penal sanctions in both Loi n° 2001-01 (Art. L96-L98, L100) and Décret n° 2001-282.

**Policy-type note:** the underlying document is a *technical standard*, not itself a legal instrument —
but the joint ministerial Arrêté that operationalises it (mandatory 6-month compliance deadline, express
sanctions, mandatory annual pollution charge) elevates it to a binding sub-legislative regulation, coded
0.75 like the implementing decree, per Coding Rule 6.

**Plastics relevance:** the standard never mentions "plastique" explicitly (confirmed by full-text
search). All 5 coded instruments qualify under filter criterion (c) — they are the technical
discharge-standard, monitoring and pollution-charge instruments that plastics-sector industrial effluent
(e.g. from manufacturing, washing, extrusion or recycling operations) is subject to in practice.

**Summary of results (Norme NS 05-061):**
- **Policy-level score (Column O)** = (0.75 + 1 + 0.75 + 0.5) / 4 = **0.75** (binding technical
  standard/ministerial order; 10 sectors ⇒ 7+ bracket; 4 of 5 life-cycle phases — production,
  consumption, disposal, environmental leakage, but not recycling; mandatory pollution charge mentioned
  but not itself declared ring-fenced within this document, so M = 0.5).
- **5 instrument rows** coded: effluent quality/discharge limit values, absolute discharge prohibitions,
  the monitoring/sampling/accreditation regime, the pollution-charge (redevance) mechanism, and the
  enhanced protection-zone regime for sensitive water bodies (e.g. Baie de Hann).
- Instrument scores (Column V) range from **0.575** (monitoring/accreditation regime) to **1.0**
  (absolute discharge prohibitions). Every instrument is coded in force (S=1); Column W flags one
  genuine borderline case (the general case-by-case zero-discharge negotiation track for existing
  dischargers into multi-use water bodies uses softer, more contingent language than the flagship
  Baie-de-Hann example).

## What was coded — Arrêtés ministériels n° 9468–9472 (2001), EIA procedure package

**Policy coded:** five ministerial orders signed the same day (28 November 2001) under reference
MJEHP-DEEC, all published together in Journal Officiel n° 6025 du 12 janvier 2002, implementing the EIA
titles of Décret n° 2001-282 (Art. R38-R44):
- **n° 9468** — public participation in the EIA process;
- **n° 9469** — organisation and functioning of the inter-ministerial "Comité technique";
- **n° 9470** — accreditation conditions for EIA consultants/firms;
- **n° 9471** — mandatory content of EIA terms of reference (the order supplied by the user as an
  uploaded PDF);
- **n° 9472** — mandatory content of the EIA report itself.

**Document completeness note:** the user's uploaded PDF contains only Arrêté n° 9471. To code the
full "regulatory package" described in the task brief (public participation, technical committee,
consultant accreditation, terms of reference, EIA report content), the full text of the other four
orders (n° 9468, 9469, 9470, 9472) was retrieved from their official mirrors (denv.gouv.sn and FAOLEX,
which host and cross-confirm all five orders as a single dated set) and coded alongside n° 9471.

**Plastics relevance:** none of the five orders mentions "plastique" explicitly. All 5 instruments
qualify under filter criterion (c) as the procedural/institutional EIA machinery that any plastics
manufacturing plant, recycling facility, landfill or incinerator must pass through before authorisation,
per the task brief. Two direct textual hooks: Arrêté n° 9471's mandatory terms-of-reference content
requires assessment of "l'élimination des déchets solides" (solid waste elimination), and Arrêté
n° 9472's mandatory report content requires a costed environmental monitoring/surveillance plan (PSE).

**Policy-type note:** all five are ministerial orders (executive branch), not Acts of Parliament, so
coded 0.75 per Rule 6 — the same tier as the implementing decree they operationalise.

**Summary of results (Arrêtés n° 9468–9472):**
- **Policy-level score (Column O)** = (0.75 + 1 + 0.75 + 0) / 4 = **0.625** (ministerial orders; 13
  sectors ⇒ 7+ bracket via the Comité technique's wide multi-sector composition; 3 of 5 life-cycle
  phases — production, disposal, environmental leakage; no budget/funding mechanism mentioned in any
  of the five orders, so M = 0).
- **5 instrument rows** coded, one per order, all Procedural measures (P = 0.40, "institutional
  setup...planning requirements...coordination") and all in force (S=1).
- Instrument scores (Column V) range from **0.45** (public-participation procedure; terms-of-reference
  content) to **0.575** (Technical Committee; consultant accreditation; EIA report content). Column W
  flags the consultant-accreditation order (n° 9470) as a borderline case that could arguably be coded
  Regulatory (1.0) instead of Procedural given its binding qualification-standard character.

## What was coded — Loi n° 2023-15 (2023), the current Code

**Policy coded:** *Loi n° 2023-15 du 02 août 2023 portant Code de l'Environnement* (Senegal) — 255
articles across 7 titles, repealing and replacing the 2001 Code (Art. 255).

Primary sources consulted (full text): the user-uploaded PDF (OCR-verified), plus the clean mirrors
https://primature.sn/sites/default/files/2023-10/Loi%20n%C2%B02023-15%20du%2002%20ao%C3%BBt%202023%20portant%20Code%20de%20l%27Environnement.pdf
and https://www.vie-publique.sn/documents/7949/loi-n-2023-15-du-02-aout-2023-portant-code-de-l-environnement-7949.

**Plastics relevance:** like the 2001 Code, the 2023 Code never mentions "plastique" / synthetic
polymers explicitly (confirmed by full-text search). All 12 coded instruments qualify under filter
criteria (b) and/or (c) — they are waste-management/governance instruments or instruments that
directly enable plastic-relevant actions (the codified EPR principle and waste hierarchy, the food-
contact recycled-packaging ban, municipal waste planning, household source-sorting) rather than
plastics-named provisions. Senegal's plastics-specific obligations remain in separate, freestanding
legislation (*Loi n° 2015-09* of 2015, replaced by *Loi n° 2020-04* of 2020), cross-referenced in
Column W.

**What's new/stronger vs. the 2001 Code** (see Column W for article-level detail):
- The **EPR principle** and the **waste hierarchy** (reuse > recycling > energy recovery > disposal)
  are now explicit, codified general principles (Art. 5, Art. 69) — the clearest general-law hook for
  plastics circularity.
- **Two ring-fenced funds** instead of one: the general Environmental Protection Fund (Art. 18) plus a
  new dedicated **marine/coastal fund (FN-PEM, Art. 19)** — directly relevant to marine plastic litter.
- A new **mandatory environmental-audit regime** (Art. 42-43) for ongoing operations, on top of the
  pre-project EIA regime.
- A new **mandatory household source-sorting duty** (Art. 89, "tri à la source") and a new **mandatory
  municipal waste-management planning obligation** (Art. 87) — neither existed in the 2001 Code.
- A new **criminal ban on using recycled waste-derived materials in food-contact packaging
  manufacture** (Art. 210) — a food-safety restriction that specifically constrains recycled-plastic
  packaging, flagged as a notable tension with the Code's own circular-economy principles.
- The old Art. L40 "waste-generating products" **enabling power** (the 2001 Code's contingent hook for
  banning items like single-use plastics, coded as *not in force*, V=0, in the 2001 table) has **no
  direct equivalent** in the 2023 Code — this role now sits with the freestanding plastics law (Loi n°
  2020-04) and the newly-binding EPR/waste-hierarchy duties (Art. 5, 65-71), which is why every
  instrument in the 2023 table is coded as **in force** (S=1).

**Summary of results (2023 Code):**
- **Policy-level score (Column O)** = (1 + 1 + 1 + 1) / 4 = **1.0** (legislation; 15 sectors ⇒ 7+
  bracket; all 5 life-cycle phases; two ring-fenced funds).
- **12 instrument rows** coded, spanning ICPE permitting, financial instruments/funds, EIA, the new
  environmental-audit regime, the EPR/waste-hierarchy duty, the open-burning ban, absolute dumping/
  hazardous-waste-import bans, municipal waste planning, household source-sorting, the recycled-
  packaging ban, State–municipality institutional coordination, and emergency response.
- Instrument scores (Column V) range from **0.45** (municipal waste planning; institutional
  coordination) to **0.875** (ICPE permitting; EPR/waste-hierarchy duty; absolute dumping bans).
  Unlike the 2001 table, no instrument scores 0 here — see the "what's new" note above.

## What was coded — Loi n° 2001-01 (2001, superseded)

**Policy coded:** *Loi n° 2001-01 du 15 janvier 2001 portant Code de l'Environnement* (Senegal).

Primary sources consulted (full text):
- https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/sen34608.pdf
- https://acbep.gouv.sn/sites/default/files/2021-12/Loi%20n%C2%B0%202001-01%20du%2012%20avril%202001%20portant%20code%20de%20l%27environnement.pdf
- https://www.sante.gouv.sn/sites/default/files/2.%2001-01-15CODENVIRONLOI.pdf
- Décret n° 2001-282 portant application du Code de l'environnement (implementing decree), for context.

**Date discrepancy note:** the task brief cited "*Loi n° 2001-01 du 12 avril 2001*". All three primary
legislative texts located show the law was deliberated by the National Assembly (29 Dec 2000) and the
Senate (4 Jan 2001) and is dated **15 January 2001**. 12 April 2001 is the date of the separate
*Décret n° 2001-282* (implementing decree for classified installations, EIA procedure, water/air/noise
pollution — issued under this law). This is flagged in Column W of the first row and in the policy
title (Column A) uses the law's own dated title.

**Supersession note:** Loi n° 2001-01 was repealed and replaced by *Loi n° 2023-15 du 2 août 2023
portant Code de l'Environnement*, adopted 7 June 2023. Per the coding rules, this table codes the 2001
law as the specific instrument requested (year = 2001, its own enactment year), since the 2023 law is a
distinct, separately-numbered replacement rather than an amendment of the 2001 text. A separate 4P Index
entry should be created for Loi n° 2023-15 if the current legal framework needs to be assessed.

**Plastics relevance:** the 2001 Code never mentions "plastique" / synthetic polymers explicitly. All
eight coded instruments qualify under filter criteria (b) and/or (c) of the coding prompt — they are
waste-management/governance instruments (classified-installations permitting, waste elimination duty,
dumping/hazardous-waste-import bans, EIA, emergency planning, State–municipality coordination) or an
enabling power (Art. L40) that directly enables future plastic-specific bans — rather than because the
Code names plastics. Senegal later exercised plastics-specific policy powers through separate,
freestanding legislation (*Loi n° 2015-09* of 4 May 2015, replaced by *Loi n° 2020-04* of 8 January
2020), which are cross-referenced in the comments column (W) and should be coded as their own policy
entries in the 4P Index rather than folded into this one.

**Summary of results (2001 Code):**

- **Policy-level score (Column O)** = (G + I + K + M) / 4 = (1 + 1 + 1 + 1) / 4 = **1.0**
  (Legislation; 13 sectors touched ⇒ 7+ bracket; all 5 life-cycle phases addressed in general terms;
  ring-fenced Environmental Protection Fund financed by the Code's own pollution levies).
- **8 instrument rows** were coded (Columns P–W), covering: classified-installations permitting,
  pollution taxes/Environmental Protection Fund, general waste-elimination duty, absolute
  dumping/hazardous-waste-import bans, the Art. L40 waste-generating-products enabling power (not yet in
  force under this law), mandatory EIA, emergency/internal-operations planning, and the
  State–municipality coordination framework.
- Instrument scores (Column V) range from **0.0** (Art. L40 enabling power, not yet operationalised) to
  **1.0** (absolute dumping/hazardous-waste-import bans).
