# 4P Index coding — São Tomé and Príncipe

## Lei n.º 09/2022 — Lei das Pescas e da Aquacultura / Law No. 09/2022 — Fisheries and Aquaculture Law

`STP_Lei-9-2022_Fisheries-Aquaculture-Law_4P-Index.csv` (and the equivalent `.xlsx` workbook)
contain the Plastic Pollution Policy Index (4P Index) coding of São Tomé and Príncipe's Fisheries
and Aquaculture Law, following the coding scheme in the task brief.

### Source note

The uploaded PDF is a 32-page **image-only scan with no extractable text layer**. The full text
(10 Chapters, 155 Articles) was transcribed by careful visual reading of each page image. This is
a genuine, verifiable document — approved by the National Assembly on 15 February 2022, promulgated
16 May 2022, and published in the Diário da República, I Série, N.º 45, of 22 June 2022 —
independently corroborated by FAOLEX, academic literature, and an FAO Development Law Service news
article.

**Amendment note:** the task brief states the law "was updated via Lei n.º 07/2025." This is
confirmed to exist (LegisPalop: "Lei n.º 7/2025 de 29 de Dezembro – Alteração e Republicação da Lei
das Pescas e da Aquacultura", Diário da República I Série N.º 56), and an FAO article describes its
scope: clarified definitions, a revised sanctions regime with fines indexed to the civil-service
minimum wage, and an extended sanctions scheme for subsistence/recreational fishing infractions
(tied to a new General Regulation on Fishing Activities, pending promulgation as of that report).
**However, the full consolidated text of Lei n.º 7/2025 could not be located for this exercise.**
Column C (`policy_year`) is coded 2025 per the amendment-year rule, but the substantive coding
(articles, fine amounts in Dobras, etc.) reflects the **original 2022 text**, since that is what was
made available. This is flagged in Columns H and W, and in the translation's translator's note and
prominent top-of-document notice.

### Explicit plastics relevance — the strongest indirect (non-explicit) link found

The word "plástico" does not appear anywhere in the law. However, **Article 139(1)(c)** prohibits
the "Abandono de artes e dispositivos de concentração não biodegradáveis nas águas marítimas
nacionais" ("Abandonment of non-biodegradable gear and concentration devices [e.g. fish-aggregating
devices] in national maritime waters") as a "serious fishing infraction." This is a direct
**Abandoned, Lost or Discarded Fishing Gear (ALDFG)** provision — ALDFG (predominantly synthetic/
plastic netting, rope and FAD material) is internationally recognised (FAO, UNEP) as one of the most
persistent and harmful sources of ocean plastic pollution. This is coded as the law's most directly
plastics-relevant instrument (Row 1).

### Coding highlights

- **10 instruments coded**: the ALDFG/gear-abandonment prohibition (Art. 139(1)(c)); the marine
  dumping/discharge prohibition for hydrocarbons, toxic/hazardous and radioactive materials (Art.
  137(1)(g),(j)); a general duty against fishing-related pollution/ecosystem degradation (Art. 22);
  regulation of at-sea works/installations and liquid-waste discharges (Art. 24); a civil-liability
  regime for environmental damage from waste/chemical dumping (Arts. 142-146); a mandatory
  environmental-accident reporting duty (Art. 144); a prohibition on destructive/prohibited fishing
  gear and methods (Art. 137(1)(k)); aquaculture effluent control (Art. 53); the multi-level
  fisheries enforcement/inspection coordination framework spanning the Directorate-General of the
  Environment, Coast Guard, Maritime and Port Institute, Local Authorities and the Príncipe Regional
  Government (Arts. 73-90); and the ring-fenced Fisheries Development Fund, partly financed by fines
  from the above infractions (Arts. 70-72).
- **Policy type = 1** (Legislation — Act of the National Assembly, Art. 97(b) of the Constitution).
- **Policy circularity = 0.75** — 4 of 5 life-cycle phases addressed (production, consumption,
  disposal, environmental leakage); recycling is not addressed.
- **Policy budget = 1** — ring-fenced via the Fisheries Development Fund (Arts. 70-72), partly
  self-financed through a percentage of fines and compensation under the Law.
- All score columns (E, G, I, K, M, P, S, T, O, V) contain **numbers only** (stored as actual
  numeric types in the `.xlsx`, not text); O and V are **calculated**, consistent with the format
  fix applied across the other STP PRs (#104–#109).

## Full English translation

`Lei_9-2022_Fisheries_Aquaculture_Law_EN_Translation.docx` contains a comprehensive,
article-by-article English translation of the full original 2022 law text (Preamble + 10 Chapters +
155 Articles), transcribed from the scanned page images, with a translator's note documenting minor
chapter-numbering inconsistencies in the original and a prominent notice about the subsequent,
unlocated Lei n.º 7/2025 amendment. This is a working translation for policy-analysis purposes, not
an official/certified legal translation — the original Portuguese text remains the sole
authoritative version.
