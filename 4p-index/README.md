# 4P Index coding — São Tomé and Príncipe

## Plano Nacional de Gestão Integrada de Resíduos Sólidos Urbanos (PNGIRSU) 2018–2023

### IMPORTANT SOURCE LIMITATION — please read before using this coding

**No government-hosted or otherwise publicly accessible copy of the actual PNGIRSU 2018-2023 plan
could be located online** for this exercise, consistent with the verification limit flagged in the
task brief. The document uploaded for this task (despite its filename) is **not** the PNGIRSU plan:
it is *"Plano de Gestão de Resíduos (PGR)"* (July 2022), an e-waste management plan prepared by
AFAP (Agência Fiduciária de Administração de Projectos) for the Ministério das Finanças da Economia
Azul, for the unrelated *"Projeto Digital de São Tomé & Príncipe"* (PD-STP) — a submarine-cable /
digital-connectivity project. That document does, however, extensively **cite and summarise**
PNGIRSU 2018-2023 data in its Sections 3–4 (legal/institutional framework and waste-management
context), attributing it to the original plan: TESE (Associação para o Desenvolvimento) (March
2018), *"Atualização do Plano Nacional de Gestão Integrada de Resíduos Sólidos Urbanos (PNGIRSU)
2018-2023"*, STP.

The coding below was therefore produced using:
1. The task brief provided for this exercise (which reports the plan's stated 60% organic-waste
   composting target and other headline features); and
2. This secondary, corroborating 2022 report's citations of PNGIRSU 2018-2023 data (waste
   composition, per-capita generation, district collection coverage, and specific eco-centre/
   composting-station/waste-processing-central initiatives).

**This coding should be treated as provisional and cross-checked against the primary PNGIRSU
2018-2023 document once obtained directly from the Ministério do Ambiente / Ministério dos
Recursos Naturais, Energia e Ambiente (MIRN).** Every row's comments column documents this sourcing
caveat explicitly.

### Coding files

`STP_PNGIRSU-2018-2023_4P-Index.csv` (and the equivalent `.xlsx` workbook) contain the Plastic
Pollution Policy Index (4P Index) coding of PNGIRSU 2018-2023, following the coding scheme in the
task brief:

- Columns A–O (policy-level) are identical across all rows.
- Columns P–W (instrument-level) vary per row; 7 instruments coded: (1) the 60% organic-waste
  composting target and composting-station network; (2) eco-centres for community waste sorting;
  (3) waste-processing centrals ("Centrais de Processamento de Resíduos") for recyclables;
  (4) selective collection of glass, cans, plastics and batteries; (5) the multi-level
  institutional/governance framework; (6) special/hazardous waste-stream planning (batteries,
  WEEE, tyres, used oils, end-of-life vehicles); and (7) the national waste-characterisation and
  data-monitoring system underlying the plan.
- Columns O (`policy_score`) and V (`instrument_score`) are calculated numeric values:
  O = average(G, I, K, M, P) and V = average(P, T), computed per row (rounded to 3 decimal places).
- All score columns (E, G, I, K, M, P, S, T, O, V) contain numbers only. Any explanation of the
  instrument-type classification (Column P) is given at the start of Column W (comments).
- Wherever original-language (Portuguese) text is quoted, an English translation is provided after
  a slash (/) separator.

**Explicit plastics references:** four of the seven coded instruments (Rows 2–4 and 7) rest on
material that explicitly and repeatedly names "plástico"/"plásticos" (plastic) — the underlying
national waste-composition survey tracks "Plásticos de todos os tipos" as a distinct category
(~5% of national waste by weight); the Caué eco-centre explicitly sorts plastic bottles; the
Lobata waste-processing central explicitly processes tonnages of "embalagens de plásticos"; and
selective collection explicitly targets plastics alongside glass, cans and batteries. This
satisfies plastics-relevance filter criterion (a) directly, and is the strongest and most frequent
explicit plastics evidence found across the São Tomé and Príncipe policies coded to date.

Policy type is coded as 0.5 (strategy/plan incorporating quantifiable targets), based on the
task brief's report of a 60% organic-waste composting target; the exact original target wording
could not be verified against the primary document (see Column F).

## Background/corroborating-source translation

`PNGIRSU_2018-2023_Background_Source_EN_Translation_Excerpts.docx` translates the PNGIRSU-relevant
excerpts (Sections 3–4) of the 2022 AFAP/PD-STP report described above — i.e., the passages that
cite, summarise or corroborate PNGIRSU 2018-2023 content — since these are the only passages of
genuine evidentiary value for this exercise. **This is not a translation of the PNGIRSU plan
itself**; the document makes this explicit in a prominent notice at the top and in its
translator's note. Portions of the source report dealing solely with the unrelated PD-STP digital
project's own e-waste management plan have been omitted as out of scope.
