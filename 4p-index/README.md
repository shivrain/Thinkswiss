# 4P Index coding — São Tomé and Príncipe

## Decreto n.º 37/1999 — Regulamento sobre o Processo de Avaliação do Impacto Ambiental / Decree No. 37/1999 — Regulation on the Environmental Impact Assessment Process

`STP_Decreto-37-1999_EIA-Decree_4P-Index.csv` (and the equivalent `.xlsx` workbook of
the same name) contain the Plastic Pollution Policy Index (4P Index) coding of São
Tomé and Príncipe's 1999 Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) Decree, following the
coding scheme in the task brief:

- Columns A–O (policy-level) are identical across all rows.
- Columns P–W (instrument-level) vary per row; one row per distinct policy instrument
  that passes the plastics-relevance filter.
- Columns O (`policy_score`) and V (`instrument_score`) are left as `[auto]` per the
  instructions and are not computed manually.
- Wherever original-language (Portuguese) text is quoted, an English translation is
  provided after a `/` separator, including for the policy name itself.

This is an executive decree (approved by the Council of Ministers, promulgated by the
President on 3 August 1999; no subsequent amendment identified) implementing Lei n.º
10/1999 (Lei de Base do Ambiente / Basic Environmental Law) by establishing a mandatory
prior Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) and environmental licensing process. It
contains no explicit reference to "plastics"; instruments were selected and scored
under the plastics-relevance filter criteria (b) and (c) — see Column W for
justification and flagged uncertainties on each of the 8 coded instruments (mandatory
EIA screening/study, public consultation, environmental licensing/precedence, licence
validity, consultant registration, monitoring/inspection/audit, sanctions/embargo, and
EIA/licensing fees).

## Full English translation

`Decreto_37-1999_EIA_Decree_EN_Translation.docx` contains a comprehensive,
article-by-article English translation of the full text of the Decree, including the
preamble and all three Annexes (list of activities requiring EIA; EIA review/licence
fee schedule; environmental-consultant registration fee schedule), plus a translator's
note flagging a chapter-numbering inconsistency present in the original Portuguese
source. This is a working translation for policy-analysis purposes, not an
official/certified legal translation — the original Portuguese text remains the sole
authoritative version.
