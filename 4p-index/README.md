# 4P Index coding — São Tomé and Príncipe

## Lei n.º 8/2020 — Sobre Medidas para a Redução do Uso de Sacos de Plástico em São Tomé e Príncipe / Law No. 8/2020 — On Measures for the Reduction of Plastic Bag Use

`STP_Lei-8-2020_Plastic-Bag-Law_4P-Index.csv` (and the equivalent `.xlsx` workbook) contain the
Plastic Pollution Policy Index (4P Index) coding of São Tomé and Príncipe's plastic bag law,
following the coding scheme in the task brief (Version 2).

### Source note

The task brief cited the Diário da Assembleia Nacional, I Série, N.º 8 (12 August 2020) — the
parliamentary proceedings record of the National Assembly's plenary approval of the bill — and
provided this as the uploaded document. That record contains the committee report detailing all
amendments adopted, which was used to cross-check the final text. However, a **more authoritative
and complete source was located and used as the primary basis for this coding**: the official,
consolidated, promulgated text of the law as published in the **Diário da República, I Série,
N.º 61 (21 September 2020), pp. 672–676** (https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/sao198723.pdf), which
contains the full 28-article text plus Annex I as enacted. These are two distinct official
gazettes: the Assembleia Nacional's own proceedings record (approval on 11 August 2020) and the
Diário da República (where the promulgated law itself — dated 4 September 2020 — was formally
published on 21 September 2020). Both are cited in Column B.

### Coding highlights

- **13 instruments coded**, reflecting the richness of this law: bans on production, importation,
  marketing, and free distribution (both private-sector and public-sector) of non-biodegradable
  plastic bags; a temporary increased import tariff; a reduced tariff and incentive framework for
  biodegradable/alternative products; awareness-raising actions; selective collection of plastic
  waste (a multi-level governance instrument, per Rule 11, spanning central government, the
  Príncipe Regional Government and Local Authorities); the multi-level supervisory/sanctioning
  competence framework; ring-fenced allocation of fine revenue (including to the Environmental
  Fund); the prohibition on littering/abandonment of plastic bags in the environment; and a
  semi-annual implementation-monitoring mechanism.
- **Policy type = 1** (Legislation — enacted by the National Assembly under Art. 97(b) of the
  Constitution).
- **Policy circularity = 1** — all five life-cycle phases are addressed (production, consumption,
  recycling — via the recycled-PET alternative and the recycling carve-out in the production ban,
  disposal, and environmental leakage).
- **Policy budget = 1** — ring-fenced via Art. 18's earmarking of 15% of all fines to the
  "Fundo do Ambiente" (Environmental Fund).
- All score columns (E, G, I, K, M, P, S, T, O, V) contain **numbers only**; O and V are
  **calculated** (O = average(G,I,K,M,P); V = average(P,T), per row), consistent with the
  score-format fix applied to the four earlier STP PRs (#104–#107).
- **Real-world enforcement caveat**: per the task brief, although the law's text uses consistently
  mandatory ("fica proibida"/"é proibido") language — scored `in_force = 1` throughout per Rule 12's
  language test — actual administrative enforcement was reportedly postponed through successive
  moratoria after the law's original 1 January 2021 entry into force, with the Ministry of
  Environment announcing full enforcement only from September 2025. This ~5-year gap between legal
  entry into force and de facto enforcement is documented in Column W for the core prohibition rows
  (1, 2, 3, 6, 12) and in the translation's translator's note, since it is a real-world
  implementation fact distinct from the textual in-force test the coding scheme applies.
- Column E (`policy_target`) is coded 0: no specific numeric reduction/recycling-rate target could
  be found in the located text, although Art. 26(e) references pre-existing "objectivos de redução
  previamente fixados" (previously set reduction objectives) without stating the figure — flagged
  in Row 8's comments as a possible cross-reference to a separate, unlocated instrument.

## Full English translation

`Lei_8-2020_Plastic_Bag_Law_EN_Translation.docx` contains a comprehensive, article-by-article
English translation of the full official law text (Preamble, 4 Chapters, 28 Articles, Annex I), as
published in the Diário da República, with a translator's note documenting both the dual-gazette
sourcing and the real-world enforcement-timeline caveat described above. This is a working
translation for policy-analysis purposes, not an official/certified legal translation — the
original Portuguese text remains the sole authoritative version.
