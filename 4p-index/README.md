# 4P Index coding — São Tomé and Príncipe

## Decreto n.º 36/1999 — Decreto Sobre Resíduos / Decree No. 36/1999 — Decree on Waste

`STP_Decreto-36-1999_Waste-Decree_4P-Index.csv` (and the equivalent `.xlsx` workbook of
the same name) contain the Plastic Pollution Policy Index (4P Index) coding of São Tomé
and Príncipe's 1999 Decree on Waste, following the coding scheme in the task brief:

- Columns A–O (policy-level) are identical across all rows.
- Columns P–W (instrument-level) vary per row; one row per distinct policy instrument
  that passes the plastics-relevance filter — 15 instruments in total.
- Columns O (`policy_score`) and V (`instrument_score`) are calculated numeric values:
  O = average(G, I, K, M, P) and V = average(P, T), computed per row (rounded to 3
  decimal places).
- All score columns (E, G, I, K, M, P, S, T, O, V) contain numbers only. Any
  explanation of the instrument-type classification (Column P) is given at the start
  of Column W (comments), e.g. "Instrument type: Regulatory (1)."
- Wherever original-language (Portuguese) text is quoted, an English translation is
  provided after a slash (/) separator, including for the policy name itself.

This is an executive decree (approved by the Council of Ministers, promulgated by the
President on 3 August 1999 — the same Council of Ministers session and signatories as
the companion EIA Decree, No. 37/1999; no subsequent amendment identified) adopting the
legal framework for deposition, collection, transport, sorting and final disposal of
municipal solid waste, and assigning core implementation responsibility to District
Chambers/municipal authorities (Art. 13).

**Explicit plastics reference:** unlike Lei n.º 10/1999 and Decreto n.º 37/1999, this
decree's mandatory Annex II waste-reporting template ("Mapa de Resíduos Sólidos")
explicitly names "Plástico" (Plastic) as a required waste-composition and
recycled-material reporting category — satisfying the plastics-relevance filter's
criterion (a) directly, in addition to (b)/(c). See Row 2 (mandatory waste inventory/
reporting obligation) for details.

**Source-quality caveat:** the only full-text copy of this decree located online
(FAOLEX) is a low-resolution scan with substantial OCR errors, especially in Articles
14–21. Coding and translation of those provisions reflect best-effort reconstruction
from legible fragments and context; this is flagged in Column W and in the
translator's note, with a recommendation to verify against the official Diário da
República text.

## Full English translation

`Decreto_36-1999_Waste_Decree_EN_Translation.docx` contains a comprehensive,
article-by-article English translation of the full text of the Decree, including the
preamble and both Annexes (list of toxic/hazardous components; the solid-waste
reporting template explicitly itemising "Plástico"), plus a translator's note
detailing the source-quality caveat above. This is a working translation for
policy-analysis purposes, not an official/certified legal translation — verification
against the official Diário da República text (Série I, No. 12 of 1999) is strongly
recommended before further reliance.
