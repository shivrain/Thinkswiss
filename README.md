## Contents

The table includes 23 instruments (laws, regulations, policies, strategies, plans, and programmes) with columns:

Policy Title (English) | Policy Title (Portuguese) | Policy Type | Last amendment year | Short description | Sectors | Life-Cycle stage | Link

The bilingual document uses direct PDF/document links only (no news articles). Most links are on official `.gov.mz` portals; where standalone texts are unavailable there, links use official Boletim da República PDFs. Four instruments without a publicly accessible direct PDF are listed with a note in the link column. International treaties are excluded.
